<p>As cool as it is to <a href="https://davidwalsh.name/html5-elements-links">wrap blocks of content inside of links</a>, it does seem to come with some <a href="http://www.html5accessibility.com/tests/blocklink.html">accessibility drawbacks</a>.</p>

            <p>A simple workaround to get the best of both worlds is to use the link’s <code>::after</code> pseudo element and place it on top of the entire block.</p>

            <p>Check out the Pen <a href="https://codepen.io/HiroAgustin/pen/VxqOGL/">Alternative Block lvl Link</a> to see the code.</p>

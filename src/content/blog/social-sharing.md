<p>There’s a simple, clean, JavaScript-less way to add social sharing links to your site: both Twitter and Facebook provide url endpoints you can use without having to load their widgets.</p>

            <ul>
              <li>
                <p><a href="https://dev.twitter.com/web/tweet-button/web-intent">Twitter’s Web Intent</a> allows you to compose a tweet from parameters.</p>
              </li>
              <li>
                <p><a href="https://developers.facebook.com/docs/workplace/integrations/sharing/#sharedialogvialink">Facebook’s Share Dialog</a> lets you set an url to be shared.</p>
              </li>
            </ul>

            <p>The tradeoff being the loss of the nice on-screen pop-up windows…</p>
